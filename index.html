The Common EU Data Spaces initiatives aim for cross border data sharing over different industry and government sectors. It depends on the ability to make data from heterogeneous sources semantically interoperable.

Data spaces are heterogeneous

  • Regional initiatives
    • local publishing baselines (OSLO)
  • Established standards
    • international interoperability(DATEXII)
  • Semantic alignment
    • bridges the gap between heterogeneous models

The data models utilized in the EU are often country and domain specific. Where some cross border standards exist at the European level, much of the available data is stuck in more direct specifications and regional modeling initiatives.
